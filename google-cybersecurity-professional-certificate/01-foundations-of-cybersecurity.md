# Course 1 — Foundations of Cybersecurity

## Overview
Introduction to the cybersecurity profession — core concepts,
security domains, frameworks, controls, and ethics that form
the foundation of all security work.

## Key Concepts Learned
- CISSP 8 Security Domains
- CIA Triad — Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework — Identify, Protect, Detect,
  Respond, Recover
- NIST RMF — 7 steps: Prepare, Categorize, Select, Implement,
  Assess, Authorize, Monitor
- Security ethics and responsible disclosure
- History of major cyberattacks

## Key Frameworks
- NIST CSF
- NIST RMF
- ISO 27001
- OWASP principles

## SOC Relevance
NIST frameworks are used daily in SOC environments to guide
incident response decisions. Understanding the RMF is essential
for understanding why controls exist and what they protect.
